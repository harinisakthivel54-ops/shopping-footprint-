🌱 Shopping Footprint

Smart Shopping. Lower Carbon. Better Choices.

Shopping Footprint is a browser extension that helps users make environmentally responsible online shopping decisions by displaying the estimated carbon emissions of products directly next to their price.

🚨 Problem

Online shoppers usually compare products based on price, ratings and reviews. However, they rarely know the environmental impact of their purchases.

💡 Our Solution

Shopping Footprint displays the estimated **CO₂e emissions** of a product before the user clicks Buy.

It also compares similar products and recommends a lower-carbon alternative. 

✨ Key Features

- 🌱 Carbon emission shown next to product price
- 📊 Eco Score for every product
- 🔄 Product carbon comparison
- 🟢 Low / 🟡 Medium / 🔴 High impact indicator
- 🌿 Lower-carbon alternative recommendation
- 📦 Carbon emission breakdown
- 📈 Personal carbon footprint dashboard
- 🎯 Monthly sustainability goals
- 💚 Carbon savings tracking

🔄 How It Works

User visits an online shopping website
↓
Shopping Footprint detects the product
↓
Carbon emission is estimated
↓
CO₂e is displayed next to the price
↓
Similar products are compared
↓
Lower-carbon alternative is recommended
↓
User makes a more sustainable choice

🌍 Carbon Footprint

Estimated product emissions can include:

- Manufacturing
- Transportation
- Packaging
- Energy / Usage
- End-of-life

🏆 Eco Score

Products are given an Eco Score based on their estimated environmental impact.

🟢 Low Impact  
🟡 Moderate Impact  
🔴 High Impact

🎯 Project Goal

Our goal is to make carbon information as easy to see as product price, helping users make informed and sustainable purchasing decisions.
